About RaagaSetu

RaagaSetu is a digital platform dedicated to preserving and promoting Indian Classical Music through technology. Our mission is to make learning ragas simple, interactive and accessible for everyone.

🎯 Our Mission

To bridge India's timeless musical heritage with modern technology and inspire the next generation of learners.

👁 Our Vision

To become India's leading digital platform for learning, listening and preserving Indian Classical Music.

💡 Key Features

  • Learning Mode
  • Listening Recommendation
  • Study Mode
  • Heritage Explorer
  • User Survey

🛠 Technologies

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Flask
