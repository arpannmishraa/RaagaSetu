Why RaagaSetu?

Indian Classical Music is one of India's richest knowledge systems, but many young people struggle to learn and understand it.

The Problem

Knowledge is scattered across books, videos and the traditional guru-shishya system, making it difficult for beginners to start.

Our Solution

RaagaSetu provides structured learning, explainable recommendations and heritage preservation through a modern digital platform.

🌿 Sustainability Goal

Preserving India's Intangible Cultural Heritage by making Indian Classical Music accessible to the next generation.
